## Delivery & Logistics

### Amazon.de to Malta
- **Eligibility:** Most pet supplies marked "Amazon.de offers" or "Fulfilled by Amazon" ship to Malta.
- **Shipping cost:** Typically €5–15 depending on item weight; free on orders €49+.
- **Delivery time:** 2–5 business days standard; longer for oversized (carrier boxes, cat trees).
- **Customs:** Malta is EU; no customs duty on purchases within EU.

### Malta Retailers (Local Pickup/Delivery)
