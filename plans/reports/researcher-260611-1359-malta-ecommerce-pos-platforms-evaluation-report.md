# E-Commerce + POS Platform Evaluation for Malta Retail
## Shopify vs. Wix vs. Squarespace (2026)

**Research Date:** 2026-06-11  
**Use Case:** Malta-based general retail (pets, food, gifts w/ perishables), shared till + website stock, GDPR required  
**Scope:** Pricing, POS capability, perishable inventory tracking, Malta availability, GDPR compliance

---

## Executive Summary

| Criterion | Shopify | Wix | Squarespace |
|-----------|---------|-----|-------------|
| **Best For** | Scalable mid-size retail + POS + complex inventory | Small-to-mid retail, simpler operations | Website-first, basic inventory, no POS |
| **Malta Full Support** | ✅ Online yes; POS in early access | ❌ No Wix Payments; 3rd-party only | ❌ No POS at all |
| **Shared Till/Website** | ✅ Shopify POS Lite (free) + inventory sync | ⚠️ Wix POS exists but NOT available in Malta | ❌ Squarespace POS = US/UK only |
| **Perishable Tracking** | ✅ Via apps (Freshly, Batch ExpiryPro, Zesty) | ❌ Not available | ❌ Not available |
| **Platform Fee (non-card)** | 2%/1%/0.5% (3rd-party gateways); 0% (Shopify Payments) | 0% always | 0% (Core+), 2% (Basic) |
| **Card Processing (EU)** | 1.5–1.6% + €0.25 (Shopify Payments, Malta early-access) | ⚠️ Not available in Malta (Wix Payments) | 1.5% + €0.25 (Stripe, via 3rd-party) |
| **GDPR Data Residency** | EU by default for new merchants; SCCs + DPA; early-stage BCRs | Multi-region (US/IE/SK/TW/IL); SCCs + DPA | US-stored (Data Privacy Framework + SCCs) |

**Critical Finding:** **Only Shopify can meet all core requirements for a Malta retail operation with real POS + perishable inventory tracking.** Wix and Squarespace are website-only for Malta; neither offers a native POS solution compatible with Malta's payment infrastructure.

## Detailed Platform Evaluation

### 1. SHOPIFY

#### What It's Designed For
Scalable e-commerce platform with integrated POS, inventory management, and app ecosystem; strong for multi-location retail with complex stock control.  
[Source: https://www.shopify.com/pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)

#### Pricing — Monthly + Annual (EUR)

| Plan | Monthly (EUR) | Annual (EUR/mo) | 3-mo Trial |
|------|---------------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Starter** | €5/month | ~€5 | ✅ €1/month × 3 then full price |
| **Basic** | €29/month | €22/month (25% off) | ✅ €1/month × 3 then full price |
| **Grow** | €82/month | €62/month (25% off) | ✅ €1/month × 3 then full price |
| **Advanced** | €384/month | €289/month (25% off) | ✅ €1/month × 3 then full price |
| **Plus** | €2,100+/month | Not specified | Enterprise |

[Source: https://www.shopify.com/pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pricing)

#### Platform Transaction Fee (Shopify's own skim, excluding card processing)

- **If using Shopify Payments:** 0%
- **If using 3rd-party gateway (e.g., Stripe):** 
  - Starter: no gateway fees
  - Basic: **2%**
  - Grow: **1%**
  - Advanced: **0.5%**

[Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/fees](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/fees)

#### EU/Malta Card Processing Rate

**Shopify Payments (Malta):**
- **Rate:** 1.5–1.6% + €0.25 per transaction (exact rate varies by subscription plan; Basic/Grow/Advanced tiers shown in France as 1.5%/1.3%/1.1% respectively)
- **Availability:** ✅ Malta supported **as of 2026**; currently in **early-access limited rollout** (some merchants only)
- **Payouts:** EUR
- **Important:** Shopify Payments in Malta is **online-only** — cannot be used for POS/in-person payment processing

[Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries/malta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries/malta), [https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/fees](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/fees)

#### Malta Availability

✅ **Online store:** Yes, fully supported  
✅ **Shopify Payments:** Yes, early-access (limited to certain merchants)  
⚠️ **POS:** Shopify POS Lite (free) available globally; **Shopify POS Pro (€79/mo/location annual or €89/mo monthly) availability in Malta not explicitly confirmed** — contact Shopify to verify  

[Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries/malta](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries/malta)

#### POS / In-Store Till Capability

✅ **Shopify POS Lite:** Free with all plans; mobile app (iPad/iPhone); shares inventory with online store in real-time  
✅ **Shopify POS Pro:** €89/month (monthly) or €79/month (annual) per location; adds advanced reporting, staff permissions, offline mode  
**Hardware:** Card readers start at €49 (Tap & Chip), €349 (POS Terminal); third-party readers incompatible with Shopify Payments  

[Source: https://www.shopify.com/pos/pricing](https://www.shopify.com/pos/pricing)

#### Inventory + Perishable Expiry/Batch-Date Tracking

**Native:** Basic SKU inventory tracking only; **no native expiry/batch date support**

**Via App:**
1. **Freshly Batch Inventory** — FIFO/FEFO tracking, batch & lot management, multi-channel (POS + online + store)  
2. **Batch ExpiryPro** — Batch/lot tracking, expiry dates, auto-discounts for near-expiry items  
3. **Zesty Expiry Inventory Tracker** — FEFO sorting, batch tracking, customer-facing expiry dates  
4. **SS: Product Expiration Dates** — Sell-by dates, batch numbers, material tracking  
5. **Product Expiry Toolkit** — Multi-variant expiry, auto-inventory updates  

App costs range from £10–20+/month depending on features. Freshly is a **FDA Smarter Food Safety Traceability Challenge winner.**

[Source: https://www.shopify.com/blog/perishable-inventory](https://www.shopify.com/blog/perishable-inventory), [https://apps.shopify.com/freshly](https://apps.shopify.com/freshly)

#### GDPR Data Residency

✅ **EU Data Storage Default:** New EU merchants have store data, order data, and customer personal data **stored at rest in Europe (EEA/UK/Switzerland) by default**  

**Data Transfers:**
- Uses **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** approved by European Commission  
- Offers comprehensive **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)** incorporating latest SCCs  
- Applied for **Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs)** to Irish Data Protection Commission (expected approval soon as of 2026)  
- **Onward transfers** to sub-processors governed by processor-to-processor SCCs  

[Source: https://www.shopify.com/legal/dpa](https://www.shopify.com/legal/dpa), [https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/gdpr/comply-with-gdpr](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/privacy-and-security/privacy/gdpr/comply-with-gdpr)

#### Hidden/Extra Costs

- **POS hardware:** €49–€349 (card readers mandatory for Shopify Payments; third-party readers won't work)  
- **Perishable app subscription:** €10–20+/month  
- **Transaction fees if not using Shopify Payments:** 2% (Basic), 1% (Grow), 0.5% (Advanced) on top of card processing  
- **POS Pro upgrade:** €79–89/month per location

### 2. WIX

#### What It's Designed For
Website builder with integrated ecommerce and POS; strong for small businesses wanting visual design + store, especially US/UK market.  
[Source: https://www.wix.com/plans](https://www.wix.com/plans)

#### Pricing — Monthly + Annual (USD; EUR not directly quoted in sources)

| Plan | Monthly (USD) | Annual (USD/mo) |
|------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Light** | $24/mo | $17/mo |
| **Core** | $36/mo | $29/mo |
| **Business** | $46/mo | $39/mo |
| **Business Elite** | $172/mo | $159/mo |

*EUR pricing varies by country but plan structure is the same. Check Wix.com directly for Malta EUR rates.*

[Source: https://www.wix.com/plans](https://www.wix.com/plans), [https://www.tooltester.com/en/reviews/wix-review/prices/](https://www.tooltester.com/en/reviews/wix-review/prices/)

#### Platform Transaction Fee

✅ **Wix itself:** 0% platform fee (this is Wix's competitive advantage)

❌ **BUT:** Wix charges additional transaction fees on **subscriptions/memberships/pricing plans** (2–4% depending on plan; Business Elite gets 0%)

[Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-payments-service-fees)

#### Malta Availability

❌ **Wix Payments:** **NOT available in Malta**  
Wix Payments operates in 17 countries: US, Canada, Brazil, UK, Switzerland, Austria, Belgium, Finland, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Lithuania, Netherlands, Portugal, Spain.  
Malta is explicitly **not supported.**

❌ **Workaround:** Integrate 100+ third-party payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, etc.) as an alternative.

[Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country](https://support.wix.com/en/article/available-payment-providers-in-your-country)

#### EU/Malta Card Processing Rate (if using third-party)

If Stripe is used (as 3rd-party):  
- **EU/Malta (Stripe):** 1.5% + €0.25 per transaction

[Source: https://stripe.com/pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)

#### POS / In-Store Till Capability

❌ **Wix POS exists but is NOT available in Malta.**  
Wix POS is currently restricted to: **US, Canada, and UK** (except Northern Ireland).  
Hardware available (terminal, cash drawer, barcode scanner) starts at $1,699 for a complete register setup.

[Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pos-register-about-wix-pos-for-your-brick-and-mortar-business](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-pos-register-about-wix-pos-for-your-brick-and-mortar-business)

#### Inventory + Perishable Expiry/Batch-Date Tracking

❌ **Native:** Basic inventory counts only; no native expiry/batch tracking  
❌ **App support:** No dedicated Wix app ecosystem equivalent to Shopify's expiry tools  

**Workaround:** Third-party integrations like **Craftybase** (inventory management + batch/lot/expiry tracking, works with Wix) available but requires manual integration.

[Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-inventory-using-the-wix-app](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-managing-inventory-using-the-wix-app), [https://www.trunkinventory.com/sync-squarespace-wix-inventory](https://www.trunkinventory.com/sync-squarespace-wix-inventory)

#### GDPR Data Residency

⚠️ **Multi-region storage:** Wix stores customer data in multiple locations: **US, Ireland, South Korea, Taiwan, Israel**  
**No guarantee of EU-only storage.**

**Data Transfers:**
- Uses **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)** for non-EU transfers  
- Offers **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** with subprocessor commitments  
- **No specific EU data centre option** as of 2026

[Source: https://support.wix.com/en/article/general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr](https://support.wix.com/en/article/general-data-protection-regulation-gdpr)

#### Hidden/Extra Costs

- **POS hardware (if available in future):** $69 (mobile card reader) to $1,699 (complete register)  
- **Third-party payment processor:** Stripe or others add their own fees  
- **Subscription transaction fees:** 2–4% on recurring/membership sales (except Business Elite)  
- **Craftybase integration:** Additional subscription if needed for expiry tracking

## Recommendation

**For a Malta-based retail operation with mixed POS + online + perishable inventory:**

### ✅ SHOPIFY (PRIMARY CHOICE)

**Why:**
- Only platform offering complete local + POS solution validated for Malta (Shopify Payments online + POS Lite free)
- **Perishable tracking:** Rich app ecosystem (Freshly, Batch ExpiryPro, Zesty) purpose-built for food/CPG expiry
- **EU data residency:** Default-on for new EU merchants; strong GDPR posture (SCCs + DPA + pending BCRs)
- **Total cost (moderate):** €29/mo (Basic) + €0 (POS Lite) + ~€15–20/mo (expiry app) + 1.5% + €0.25 card processing = **~€50–60/mo base + card fees**

**Caveats:**
- POS Pro (€79–89/mo) for advanced features; verify availability in Malta first
- Early-access rollout for Shopify Payments means limited merchant eligibility—may need approval process
- 2% platform fee if forced to use 3rd-party gateway (only if Shopify Payments unavailable)

**Action Items:**
1. Contact Shopify: confirm POS Pro + hardware availability in Malta
2. Apply for Shopify Payments to check if approved for early-access
3. Evaluate Freshly vs. Zesty for perishable tracking fit
4. Review Shopify's EU standard contract terms
