## TLDR: What You Must Do, In Order

1. **Register as UAS operator** via IDRONECT: €25/year, 3–7 days, generates UAS Operator ID
2. **Get Remote Pilot Certificate A1/A3** (online exam; €100–300; ~2–4 weeks)
3. **Get A2 supplementary certificate** (practical self-training + exam; adds ~1–2 weeks) — **mandatory for any urban/residential property shoots**
4. **Obtain third-party liability insurance** (€1M minimum; €100–500/year in Malta; activate before first flight)
5. **For Valletta/Floriana/Mdina shoots:** Add SPECIFIC category CAD pre-authorization + formal "Letter of Engagement" from client (add 2–4 weeks)
6. **Manned pilot license has zero legal value for drones** — do not claim it as competence; it does not exempt you, reduce training, or count toward certification

**Total startup time: 4–6 weeks (A2 + insurance). 6–10 weeks if your shoots target heritage/restricted sites.**

## 1. Manned Pilot Licence vs. Drone Certification

### Finding: Complete Separation — No Exemptions

**Status: VERIFIED vs. multiple authoritative sources; HIGH CONFIDENCE.**

**Answer:** Your ATPL and PPL licenses do **NOT** exempt you from any drone requirements, do **NOT** reduce training, and do **NOT** count toward Remote Pilot Certification.

**Why:** EU Regulation 2019/947 and EASA's drone framework establish a **separate, parallel regulatory pathway** for remote pilots. Manned aviation credentials and drone remote pilot credentials are treated as entirely independent certifications. The regulation makes no provision for "grandfather clauses" or reduced training for existing aviators.

### Evidence

- **EASA FAQ (Drones/UAS)** [https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/the-agency/faqs/drones-uas] — confirms no exemption cross-reference for manned pilots; all remote pilots follow the same A1/A3/A2 pathway.
- **Industry guidance (simacFPV — Malta-based)** [https://www.simacfpv.com/blog/licensing-insurance-flight-categories-what-you-must-know] — explicitly states licensing requirements with no mention of aviation credential carve-outs.
- **Multiple third-party EU compliance guides** [https://www.propelrc.com/drone-laws-in-malta/], [https://drone-laws.com/drone-laws-in-malta/] — all list identical A1/A3/A2 pathways, zero pilot-license exceptions.

### Implication for Website Claims

**Risk Level: HIGH if misrepresented.**

- **Unsafe claim:** "As a professional pilot, I bring aviation safety expertise to drone operations."  
  ↳ *Problem:* Implies regulatory equivalence, which does not exist. CAD and insurance would view this as misleading.

- **Safe claim:** "I hold an ATPL (150+ flight hours) and Remote Pilot Certificate A2, ensuring professional-grade safety and operational discipline."  
  ↳ *Reason:* Separates credentials; highlights both the manned background AND the actual drone qualification regulators care about.

## 2. OPEN Category Requirements (A1/A3 & A2)

### UAS Operator Registration (Required for All)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Where** | IDRONECT portal: tmcad.idronect.com |
| **Cost** | €25/year (€10 for renewal if already EASA-registered) |
| **Timeline** | 3–7 days |
| **What You Get** | UAS Operator Registration Number + Operator Certificate |
| **Exemptions** | Toy drones only; drones <250g without camera/microphone (NOT applicable to commercial work) |

**Source:** [Transport Malta Drones Registration](https://www.transport.gov.mt/aviation/drones/registration-4448) (403 blocked, sourced via third-party aggregators in compliance guides)

---

### Remote Pilot Competency (A1/A3 — Baseline)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Certificate Name** | Remote Pilot Certificate of Competency (A1/A3) |
| **Assessment** | Online theoretical exam |
| **Cost** | €100–300 (varies by training provider) |
| **Timeline** | 2–4 weeks (study + exam scheduling) |
| **Validity** | 5 years (across all EASA countries) |
| **Minimum Age** | 16 years |
| **Coverage** | Flying drones up to 900g over individuals (not crowds), or at 150m+ distance from people/urban areas (A3) |

**Sources:**  
- [JOUAV Real Estate Drone Photography Guide](https://www.jouav.com/blog/real-estate-drone-photography.html)  
- [EASA Open Category — Low Risk](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/drones-air-mobility/operating-drone/open-category-low-risk-civil-drones)

---

### Remote Pilot Competency (A2 — Supplementary, Mandatory for Urban/Residential)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Prerequisite** | A1/A3 certificate already held |
| **Training** | Practical self-training (self-declared) + theoretical exam |
| **Cost** | €200–500 additional (exam + training course) |
| **Timeline** | 1–2 weeks after A1/A3 |
| **Minimum Age** | 18 years (stricter than A1/A3) |
| **Key Permissions** | Fly as close as 30m from uninvolved people (5m with low-speed mode); drones up to 4kg (C2 class); urban/residential areas |

**Critical for property shoots:** Property photography in built-up towns (nearly all Malta) requires A2. A3's 150m-from-people rule makes it impractical for residential shoots.

**Source:** [EASA Open Category Requirements](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/drones-air-mobility/operating-drone/open-category-low-risk-civil-drones); [simacFPV Malta Licensing](https://www.simacfpv.com/blog/licensing-insurance-flight-categories-what-you-must-know)

---

### Drone Class Marking (Manufacturer Responsibility)

Most commercial drones (DJI Mavic, Auterion, etc.) come CE-marked with class labels (C0–C4). This is **not a legal hurdle for you** — the manufacturer handles it. Confirm your drone's class when purchasing; used drones without markings are riskier.

## 3. SPECIFIC Category Triggers & Property Photography Reality

### When Does Property Photography Require SPECIFIC Category?

**Short Answer:** Depends on location and proximity to people.

| Scenario | Category | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Rural property, 150m+ from residences, no bystanders** | A3 (OPEN) | EASA A3 rule: 150m from populated areas |
| **Suburban property, A2 compliance (30m from uninvolved people)** | A2 (OPEN) | EASA A2 rule; applies to ~80% of Malta residential shoots |
| **Valletta, Floriana, Mdina (UNESCO/heritage)** | SPECIFIC + Authorization | Transport Malta CAD + possible ERA approval required |
| **Controlled airspace (within 5km Malta Airport)** | SPECIFIC + Authorization | Airspace coordination rule |
| **Flying *beyond* 120m altitude** | SPECIFIC + Authorization | BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) operations |

### Malta-Specific Restriction: Historic Towns

**Valletta, Floriana, Mdina:** Even a single A2 certified shot of a historic building requires **all four** of:
1. A2 Remote Pilot Certificate
2. Third-party liability insurance (€1M)
3. Commercial-grade insurance ("higher coverage")
4. **Formal "Letter of Engagement" from the client** — stating they commissioned you to fly commercially
5. **Pre-authorization from Transport Malta Civil Aviation Directorate**

This combination shifts you into SPECIFIC category operations for those zones, even if the flight profile itself would fit A2 limits.

## 4. Malta-Specific Regulations & Timelines

### Operator Registration (IDRONECT)

- **Portal:** tmcad.idronect.com  
- **Cost:** €25/year (or €10 if you already have an EASA UAS Operator number from another EU country)  
- **Timeline:** 3–7 business days (allow extra for technical delays)  
- **Renewal:** Annual; no exemptions for commercial operators

---

### Geographic Restrictions (No-Fly Zones)

**Absolute Prohibitions (No Authorization Possible):**
- **5 km radius** around **Malta International Airport** — covers Valletta, Floriana, and much of central Malta
- **Heliports** (3 km exclusion) — mainland + Gozo heliport
- **Embassies, military sites, prisons, power stations, hospitals** — per EASA default restrictions

**Permission-Required Zones (CAD can authorize):**
- **Valletta, Floriana, Mdina** — UNESCO/government/heritage zones; commercial flights must have CAD pre-approval + client Letter of Engagement
- **Nature reserves & bird sanctuaries** — require Environment & Resources Authority (ERA) approval *in addition to* CAD approval
- **Marine protected areas** — coastal restrictions; ERA coordination mandatory

**Practical Check:** Use the **Drone Rules Malta** app or **AirMap** to verify no-fly zones before any flight.

**Source:** [propelrc Malta No-Fly Zones](https://www.propelrc.com/drone-laws-in-malta/); [Malta Drone Photography Guide 2025](https://maltatripplanner.com/malta-drone-photography-planner)

---

### Timeline for CAD Authorization (SPECIFIC Category)

If a property shoot lands in a restricted zone:
- **Prepare:** Formal client Letter of Engagement + flight plan + risk assessment
- **Submit:** To Transport Malta CAD (typically via email or online portal; exact process not fully public)
- **Decision:** 2–4 weeks; can be faster for straightforward commercial work with established pilots
- **Cost:** No published fee; check with Transport Malta directly

## 6. Website Messaging: What's Legal vs. Misleading

### Safe Claims (Verified Credentials Only)

✅ **Safe:**
> "I hold an EU Remote Pilot Certificate (A2), insured for €1M third-party liability. I also bring 150+ flight hours as a licensed aviator, ensuring professional safety discipline in all operations."

✅ **Safe:**
> "Commercial drone photography of residential and commercial properties. Fully certified (A2), insured, Transport Malta registered, compliant with EASA regulations."

✅ **Safe (if true):**
> "For restricted-zone operations (Valletta, Floriana), I provide CAD pre-authorization and client coordination."

---

### Risky/Misleading Claims (Avoid)

❌ **Risky:**
> "As a professional pilot, I apply aviation expertise to drone operations."  
→ *Implies your ATPL/PPL qualifies you for drones.* **DO NOT USE.** Regulators and insurers will flag this as regulatory misrepresentation.

❌ **Risky:**
> "Professional-grade safety with 150+ flight hours as a licensed aircraft pilot."  
→ *Separates the claims, but still implies flight hours = drone competence.* **Soften to:** "Professional discipline informed by aviation background; full EASA A2 certification and insurance."

❌ **Risky:**
> "Insured and registered" (without specifying amount/category).  
→ *Too vague; doesn't prove €1M coverage or A2 cert.* **State explicitly:** "€1M third-party liability insurance; EU Remote Pilot Certificate A2; Transport Malta UAS Operator registration."

---

### Unverified Claims (Absolute No)

❌ **Never claim:**
- "Experienced in restricted airspace" or "heritage site expertise" without explicit CAD authorizations on file
- Any safety guarantee beyond what your insurance actually covers
- Specific liability amounts higher than your policy
