2. Larger groups (AX, db, Eden, Tal-Lira, The Atrium) may have more procurement muscle than surfaced — qualify on first call.
3. Eden Leisure + db Group have brand-standard properties (InterContinental, incoming Hard Rock) — pitch only their own-brand estate.
4. Named decision-makers exist for 3 rows only (Hugo's, Eden Leisure, Marvin Gauci); elsewhere open via generic inbox and ask for owner/purchasing.
5. Freshness flags: Busy Bee's rebuilt site serves noindex; Calypso + Sphinx mid-website-rebuild; Customize Nation's tel: link is a placeholder (displayed number recorded).
6. iGaming was the weakest segment despite Malta's sector size — operators hide contacts behind press forms; if pursued, go via LinkedIn/personal networks, not scraped contacts.
