Should you use Shopify?

Great at: easiest fully-managed shop for a non-coder — hosting/SSL/PCI/security done for you, best checkout (Shop Pay), real POS + stock sync, huge app store, built-in AI, scales to enterprise.

Bites: real cost is 2–3× the plan (apps+fees, ~€120–250/mo not €22); locked to Shopify Payments (2/1/0.6% surcharge otherwise); payout-hold risk; migration is painful (Liquid + URL lock-in); weaker blog/SEO than WordPress.

Verdict (Malta non-coder shop): still the best all-round pick for online + simple in-store — if you budget realistically, use Shopify Payments (0% cut), and confirm the in-person Malta flag below.

Match & verdict

Who it's for — and the call

Shines when…

  • Non-coder who wants to sell, not sysadmin
  • Online + simple in-store, one stock
  • Under ~€500k/mo, OK with ~1–2% platform tax
  • Values reliability, checkout, support

    • Look elsewhere when…

  • Content/SEO-led brand → WooCommerce/WordPress
  • Very thin margins (app+fee creep hurts)
  • Complex products / chargeback-prone (hold risk)
  • €1M+/mo → BigCommerce / self-hosted Woo
    • Use Shopify if you want the easiest, fully-managed way to run a Malta shop online + in-store as a non-coder — accepting ~€120–250/mo real cost, Shopify Payments for 0% cut, and that it's a long-term home (migration is painful).
    Settle two things first: (1) confirm in-person card processing works in Malta at signup; (2) know the payout-hold risk exists so a big month / chargeback spike doesn't blindside you.
