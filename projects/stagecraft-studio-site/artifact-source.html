Staging or styling?

Virtual staging furnishes an empty room so a listing photographs well. It is for selling or letting, and it is priced per image.

Styling & decor is for the owner: how the space could actually look once it is dressed. Same craft, different purpose, and it comes with a list of what is in the picture.

Decorating, not renovating

This is furniture, lighting, art, plants and textiles. We do not repaint walls, change flooring or move anything structural, and we will not pretend a room has been renovated when it has not.

Want it done for real rather than in the photo? On-location styling is an add-on to any property shoot. We dress the space on the day, then photograph it.

Send photographs by WhatsApp or a shared link. Nothing else needed. Every staged or styled image is delivered labelled as such, which is what property portals require and what keeps a listing honest.
