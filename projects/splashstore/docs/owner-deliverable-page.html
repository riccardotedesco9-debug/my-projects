Five rules that keep it working
Open the Shopify app before entering the garage. Opened on mobile data first, it keeps working even in a dead spot and syncs later.
Record every cash/Revolut sale the same day. Unrecorded sales are the #1 reason shop stock counts drift wrong.
Electronics never sleep in the chlorine garage. Chlorine vapour corrodes them. Scan in there for minutes; store and charge in the clean garage or at home.
Battery protection on, day one. Samsung's 80–85% charge cap — tablets that live on chargers in hot rooms die of swollen batteries in ~2 years.
Test before the big scan. In the first week (while returns are possible): scanner adds items to the Shopify cart, and its offline memory uploads correctly.

One practical note on ordering: everything should be tested together in the first week — scanner to tablet, offline scanning, label printing — while the shops' return windows are still open. Ask iLabMalta to quote the DS2278's charging cradle with the order (drop-in charging plus a wired backup connection).

The chlorine rule (worth repeating)

Chlorine fumes rust and destroy electronics, tools and shelving. Keep chemicals in their own ventilated garage with lids tight; keep the tablet, scanner, printer and chargers in the other garage or at home. This one habit protects the whole kit.
