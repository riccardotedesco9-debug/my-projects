# Turtle Adopt-Me Poster

Domain: WebDesign

A4 portrait "Adopt Me" poster for a box turtle in the shop. Bold & punchy style,
real cut-out turtle photo as hero, CSS-designed for vector-crisp print text.
Output: print-ready PNG + PDF at 300 DPI in `out/`.

- `poster.html` / `poster.css` — the design (edit copy + contact details here)
- `assets/turtle-hero.png` — cleaned, cut-out, upscaled turtle (the hero image)
- `assets/turtle-raw.png` — original photo (612x408, plain white bg)
- Contact details on the poster are **placeholders** — swap for the real ones, then re-render.

Re-render: open `poster.html` in a browser, or use Chrome DevTools to screenshot the
`.poster` element at high DPR and print-to-PDF (A4, no margins, background graphics on).
